Satisfy: Cozy ASMR Puzzles – Privacy Policy

Developer: AAstudiosUY · Last updated: November 7, 2026

Satisfy is an offline puzzle game. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or any other personal content. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and produce analytics, the AdMob SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions (for example, ad views and taps)
  • Diagnostics, crash logs and other app performance data

This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads / Privacy › Ads). Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Children

Satisfy is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any data on our own servers. Uninstalling the app deletes all local game data. For data held by Google, see the links above.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
